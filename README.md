## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
Proposed Solution

We propose to develop a digital agricultural marketplace that directly connects farmers and Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs) with consumers, retailers, restaurants, and bulk buyers.

The platform will allow farmers/FPOs to list their agricultural products with details such as quantity, quality, expected price, location, and availability. Buyers can directly view the available products and place orders without depending on multiple intermediaries.

The platform will also provide logistics support by coordinating transportation and delivery. AI-based demand forecasting will analyse previous sales and market data to predict future demand. Route optimization will help identify efficient delivery routes, reducing transportation time and cost.

The main objective is to increase farmers' earnings, provide better prices to consumers, reduce unnecessary intermediaries, minimize post-harvest losses, and create a transparent agricultural supply chain.

## Technical Approach

Technical Approach

The proposed system will be developed as a web/mobile-based digital marketplace with separate interfaces for farmers/FPOs, buyers, and logistics providers.

Technologies

- Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript / React
- Backend: Python with Django
- Database: MySQL / PostgreSQL
- AI/ML: Python, Pandas, Scikit-learn
- Maps and Routing: Map API and route optimization algorithms
- Cloud: Cloud-based hosting and storage

Working Process

1. Farmers/FPOs register and upload their available agricultural products.
2. Farmers provide product details such as quantity, quality, price, location, and availability.
3. Consumers or bulk buyers search and purchase the required products.
4. The system matches available products with buyer requirements.
5. Orders are confirmed and logistics support is arranged.
6. AI analyses historical and current data to forecast product demand.
7. The system uses route optimization to identify efficient transportation routes.
8. The buyer receives the product directly from the farmer/FPO through the supported logistics network.

The system will provide dashboards for farmers, buyers, and administrators to monitor products, orders, prices, demand, and deliveries.

## Impact and Benefit
Feasibility and Viability

The proposed solution is technically feasible because it can be developed using widely available web technologies, cloud services, databases, AI/ML tools, and map-based services.

The platform can initially be implemented in selected regions with a limited number of farmers, FPOs, buyers, and logistics partners. After successful testing, it can be scaled to additional regions and agricultural products.

Potential Challenges

- Farmers may have limited digital knowledge.
- Maintaining accurate product and price information may be difficult.
- Transportation and delivery coordination can be challenging.
- Demand forecasting requires sufficient and reliable data.
- Ensuring secure transactions and user data is important.

Strategies

The platform will provide a simple and multilingual user interface, assisted onboarding for farmers, data validation, secure authentication, and continuous monitoring. The AI models can be improved as more transaction and demand data becomes available.
